OuterProduct is building the infrastructure to enable unified reasoning for enterprise structured data and empower enterprise AI with verifiable reasoning context. 

At our core is the Unified Reasoning Engine. We've built a platform that allows users to connect and query over multiple sources of data, train machine learning models, and reason over this intelligence, all with full governance and on managed infrastructure. 

At a high level, there are two core objects around which the engine and corresponding SDK functions are built around:

- **DataFrame**: derived from queries over the universe of your data (S3, Snowflake, Databricks, or local tables).
- **Reasoning:** train a **reasoning model** optimized for reasoning across multiple model families (including tabular foundation models), hyper-parameter configurations, and metrics. Reasoning models come with native reasoning across the structured data stack.

We will explain both in detail as we guide you through the end-to-end functionalities of the platform.

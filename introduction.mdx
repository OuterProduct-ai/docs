OuterProduct gives you a small, typed Python surface for AI reasoning on structured data — training models, generating predictions, and producing feature-level reasoning — all without managing infrastructure. You bring a structured dataset and a label column; OuterProduct handles model search, training, and the reasoning that grounds every decision.

## Key capabilities

OuterProduct centers on a handful of composable building blocks:

- **ReasoningModel** — trains a model that produces both predictions *and* feature-level reasoning for each prediction. This is the flagship capability.
- **Trainer** — trains a model optimized for predictive accuracy when you need predictions only and don't need the reasoning layer.
- **PatternTracker** — aggregates local reasoning across a population to surface recurring patterns and segment-level drivers.
- **Counterfactuals** — generate "what would need to change?" scenarios for any prediction, enabling actionable recommendations.
- **Connectors** — load data directly from S3, Snowflake, or Databricks, or upload a local CSV or Parquet file. All connectors produce a typed `Dataset` handle.
- **MCP server** — use OuterProduct from Claude, Cursor, and any other Model Context Protocol client without writing Python directly.

## How it fits your workflow

OuterProduct sits between your raw structured data and the decisions you want to make with it. You point it at a dataset and a label column, call `fit()`, and get back a model object. From there you call `predict()` for scores and `explain()` for the reasoning behind them — both return structured, typed results you can log, visualize, or pass downstream.

Training runs asynchronously on OuterProduct's hosted infrastructure. The `.wait()` helper blocks your script until the model is ready, or you can poll with `job.status()` and integrate the job into your own orchestration.

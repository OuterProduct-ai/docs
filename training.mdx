title: "Training"
---

`op.reasoning.fit()` submits the training job with your task, HPO space, and optimizer. It returns the Pareto frontier of models over the metrics you choose to optimize. Call `.wait()` to block until results are ready.

```python
job = op.reasoning.fit(
    train_tbl.drop(split_id),        # all features except the ID column
    task=task,
    metrics=["auc", "neg_class_error"],
    hpo_space=hpo_space,
    optimizer=optimizer,
    random_state=42,
)
curve = job.wait()
```

**Supported metrics**

- `auc` — area under the ROC curve
- `neg_class_error` — negative misclassification rate
- `neg_rmse` — negative RMSE
